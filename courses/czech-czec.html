Source: Yale College Programs of Study 2026–2027 (Bulletin of Yale University, Series 122, No. 1, May 5, 2026; closing date for material March 20, 2026). Converted to plain text for an advising chatbot. Yale may amend this information at any time; for current course times, instructors, and offerings, students should check Yale Course Search (https://courses.yale.edu) and confirm with their residential college dean or the relevant DUS.
What this page contains: course descriptions for one subject. Each course is a self-contained block: Course (all cross-listed numbers), Title, Subject, Term, Instructor, Designations, Course credit (only when different from 1), Description, and Prerequisite (when listed). Cross-listed courses appear under every subject that lists them. Term is inferred from the course-number suffix: "a" = fall, "b" = spring. "(*)" reproduces an asterisk printed before the course number in the catalog; the catalog does not define it in its key.
