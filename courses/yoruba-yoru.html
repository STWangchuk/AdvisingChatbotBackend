YORU 1110a — Beginning Yorùbá I

Course: YORU 1110a

Title: Beginning Yorùbá I

Subject: Yoruba (YORU)

Term: Fall

Instructor: Oluseye Adesola

Description: Training and practice in speaking, listening, reading, and writing. Initial emphasis is on the spoken aspect, with special attention to unfamiliar consonantal sounds, nasal vowels, and tone, using isolated phrases, set conversational pieces, and simple dialogues. Multimedia materials provide audio practice and cultural information. L1 1½ Course cr

YORU 1130a — Intermediate Yorùbá I

Course: YORU 1130a

Title: Intermediate Yorùbá I

Subject: Yoruba (YORU)

Term: Fall

Instructor: Oluseye Adesola

Description: Refinement of students' speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills. More natural texts are provided to prepare students for work in literary, language, and cultural studies as well as for a functional use of Yorùbá. After YORU 120. L3 1½ Course cr

YORU 1150a — Advanced Yorùbá I

Course: YORU 1150a

Title: Advanced Yorùbá I

Subject: Yoruba (YORU)

Term: Fall

Instructor: Oluseye Adesola

Designations: L5 (Language level 5 (beyond L4))

Description: An advanced course intended to improve students’ aural and reading comprehension as well as speaking and writing skills. Emphasis on acquiring a command of idiomatic usage and stylistic nuance. Study materials include literary and nonliterary texts; social, political, and popular entertainment media such as movies and recorded poems (ewì); and music. After YORU 140.

YORU 1170a — Topics in Yorùbá Literature and Culture

Course: YORU 1170a

Title: Topics in Yorùbá Literature and Culture

Subject: Yoruba (YORU)

Term: Fall

Instructor: Oluseye Adesola

Designations: L5 (Language level 5 (beyond L4)), HU (Humanities & Arts)

Description: Advanced readings and discussion concerning Yorùbá literature and culture. Focus on Yorùbá history, poetry, novels, movies, dramas, and oral folklore, especially from Nigeria. Insight into Yorùbá philosophy and ways of life.

Prerequisite: YORU 160.
