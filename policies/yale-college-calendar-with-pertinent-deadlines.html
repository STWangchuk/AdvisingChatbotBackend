Yale College Calendar with Pertinent Deadlines

Source: Yale College Programs of Study 2026–2027 (Bulletin of Yale University, Series 122, No. 1, May 5, 2026; closing date for material March 20, 2026). Converted to plain text for an advising chatbot. Yale may amend this information at any time; for current course times, instructors, and offerings, students should check Yale Course Search (https://courses.yale.edu) and confirm with their residential college dean or the relevant DUS.

This calendar includes a partial summary of deadlines given in the Academic Regulations and in the Yale College online publication Undergraduate Regulations. Unless otherwise specified, references are to sections in the Academic Regulations, and deadlines fall at 5 p.m. (EST). The instructional week begins on Monday and ends on Sunday.

Link to the 2025–2026 YC Calendar of Pertinent Deadlines.

Dates are subject to change.

FALL 2026

Aug. 17 (M): Registration opens for first-year students, 12 p.m. and remains open until the close of add/drop period.

Aug. 23 (Su): Residences open to new students, 9:00 a.m.

Aug. 26 (W): Add/drop period opens, 8:00 a.m.

Aug. 30 (Su): Residences open to upper-level students, 9:00 a.m.

Sept. 1 (T): Required check-in meetings for upper-level students (Class of 2029, 9:00 a.m.; Class of 2028, 9:45 a.m.; Class of 2027, 10:30 a.m.)

Sept. 2 (W): Fall classes begin.

Sept. 4 (F): Friday classes do not meet; Monday classes meet instead.

Sept. 7 (M): Labor Day; classes do not meet.

Sept. 11 (F): Add/drop period ends, 5:00 p.m.

Sept. 15 (T): All students planning to complete degree requirements at the end of the fall term must file a petition by this date.

Sept. 15 (T): Students in their final term of enrollment must petition to complete the requirements of two majors by this date.

Sept. 15 (T): Students in their final term of enrollment planning to change their major, must by this date, declare as such in Yale Hub.

Sept. 15 (T): Students in their final term of enrollment planning to earn a certificate, must by this date, declare as such in Yale Hub.

Sept. 16 (W): Final deadline to apply for fall-term Leave of Absence.

Sept. 16 (W): Withdrawal from Yale College on or before this date entitles a student to a full rebate of fall-term tuition (Undergraduate Regulations).

Sept. 24 (Th): Last day to withdraw from a course offered in the first half of the fall term without the course appearing on the transcript.

Sept. 26 (S): Withdrawal from Yale College on or before this date entitles a student to a rebate of one-half of fall-term tuition. See Undergraduate Regulations.

Oct. 1 (Th): Applications close for spring 2027 Term Abroad.

Oct. 16 (F): Last day of courses offered in the first half of the fall term.

Oct. 16 (F): Last day to withdraw from a course offered in the first half of the fall term.

Oct. 16 (F): Last day to convert from a letter grade to the Cr/D/F option in a course offered in the first half of the fall term.

Oct. 19 (M): Courses offered in the second half of the fall term begin.

Oct. 20 (T): October recess begins after last academic obligation.

Oct. 20 (T): Midterm.

Oct. 20 (T): Last day to withdraw from a fall full-term course without the course appearing on the transcript.

Oct. 20 (T): Deadline to apply for double credit in a single-credit course.

Oct. 20 (T): Withdrawal from Yale College on or before this date entitles a student to a rebate of one-quarter of the term's tuition.

Oct. 20 (T): Final ABX exam grades are due.

Oct. 26 (M): Classes resume.

Nov. 12 (Th): Last day to withdraw from a course offered in the second half of the fall term without the course appearing on the transcript.

Nov. 13 (F): Spring term registration opens for Graduate School students, 8:00 a.m.

Nov. 16 (M): Spring term registration opens for Class of 2027, 8:00 a.m.

Nov. 17 (T): Spring term registration opens for Class of 2028, 8:00 a.m.

Nov. 18 (W): Spring term registration opens for Class of 2029, 8:00 a.m.

Nov. 19 (Th): Spring term registration opens for Class of 2030, 8:00 a.m.

Nov. 20 (F): November recess begins after last academic obligation.

Nov. 30 (M): Last day to relinquish on-campus housing for the spring term without charge (Undergraduate Regulations).

Nov. 30 (M): Classes resume.

Dec. 11 (F): Yale College classes end; reading period begins.

Dec. 11 (F): Last day to convert from a letter grade to the Cr/D/F option for a full-term course and/or a course offered in the second half of the fall term.

Dec. 11 (F): Last day to withdraw from a full-term course and/or a course offered in the second half of the fall term.

Dec. 14 (M): Graduate School classes end.

Dec. 15 (T): Final examinations begin. No final exams on Saturday, Dec 19 or Sunday, Dec 20.

Dec. 20 (Su): Deadline for all course assignments, other than term papers and term projects. This deadline can be extended only by a Temporary Incomplete (TI) authorized by the student’s residential college dean.

Dec. 22 (T): Deadline for all term papers and term projects. This deadline can be extended only by a Temporary Incomplete (TI) authorized by the student’s residential college dean.

Dec. 22 (T): Final examinations end. Winter recess begins.

Dec. 22 (T): Official degree conferral date for fall 2026. Degree clearance begins once final grades have been submitted.

Dec. 22 (T): Spring term registration closes for all GSAS & YC students, 5:00 p.m.

Dec. 23 (W): Residences close, 12 noon.

May 5 (W): Final examinations begin. No final exams on Saturday, May 8 or Sunday, May 9.

May 9 (Su): Deadline for all course assignments, other than term papers and term projects. This deadline can be extended only by a Temporary Incomplete (TI) authorized by the student’s residential college dean.

May 19 (W): Final grades due for continuing students.

May 24 (M): University Commencement.

May 25 (T): Residences close for seniors, 12 noon.
